# PROJECT.md — SIH26135 Skilling Intelligence Platform

## 1. Problem statement (SIH26135)

> Difficulties in tracking employment outcomes, skill gaps, and the impact of skilling initiatives.

Government and training organisations run many skilling initiatives, but it is difficult to answer three questions with structured evidence:

1. **Employment outcomes** — What happens to trainees after training? Who gets a job, in a relevant role, and does the job last?
2. **Skill gaps** — Where does labour-market demand exceed the supply of trained skills? Which skills should be prioritised, and in which districts?
3. **Programme impact** — Which skilling programmes actually convert training into employment, and which underperform?

This project turns trainee / training / employment / job-market (labour-demand) **demo** data into structured evidence for a government decision dashboard.

---

## 2. Project goals

- Build a **working, demonstrable prototype** in **6 days** that a college student team can build & explain.
- Connect the data flow: **data entry → database → analytics → government dashboard**.
- Keep the architecture **simple and modular** (FastAPI + SQLite + pandas + React).
- Use **synthetic demo data** only; never claim a real government integration.
- Include AI/LLM **only** as an optional, post-core explanation layer — never as the source of statistics.

---

## 3. User roles

| Role | Who they are | What they can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Admin / Government** | Decision-maker | Dashboard, employment outcomes, skill-gap analysis, district views, programme impact, reports |
| **Training Provider** | Runs programmes | Manage training programmes; view trainee statistics, completion, certification, outcomes |
| **Trainee** | Person being trained | Profile, education, skills, training history, certifications, employment status/details |

**Auth model:** Demo accounts are **seeded** by `scripts/seed_demo_data.py`. There is **no public registration** in the prototype. Access is enforced by a role field on the user + JWT.

---

## 4. Core data (all demo/synthetic)

| Domain | Fields (planned) |
| --- | --- |
| **Trainee** | trainee_id, education, district, skills |
| **Training** | program_id, program_name, provider, skills_taught, duration, completion_status, certification_status |
| **Employment** | trainee_id, employment_status, job_role, industry, salary, employment_start_date, retention info |
| **Job-market (labour demand)** | job_role, required_skill, industry, district, demand |

---

## 5. Core analytics

1. **Employment outcomes** — completion rate, employment rate, relevant-employment rate, retention rate.
2. **Skill gaps** — compare labour demand with available trained skills; identify high-demand/low-supply skills; district-wise.
3. **Programme impact** — compare programmes on employment, relevant employment, retention; rank high- and low-performing programmes.

> "Impact" is measured as **outcome comparison between programmes** (correlation), never claimed as strict causal proof. This is stated so the presentation is rigorous.

---

## 6. Demo-data & honesty policy

- All data is **synthetic**, generated by `scripts/seed_demo_data.py`.
- Inputs live under `data/raw/`; the generated prototype DB under `data/processed/`.
- **No external government API** is called; **no real dataset is claimed**. Any future real-data integration is out of scope.
- The LLM (P2) may rephrase or explain **only numbers provided by the application's analytics** — it must never invent statistics.

---

## 7. Scope: in vs out (6 days)

**In (MVP):** auth w/ seeded accounts, role-based access, trainee/training/employment data (minimal CRUD), SQLite DB, employment/skill-gap/program-impact analytics, government dashboard, demo data.

**Out (postponed / optional):** predictive ML, blockchain, microservices, Kafka/Redis/Docker/Kubernetes, real data integrations, AI assistant (P2 — only after core works), extensive reporting/export, extra charts.
